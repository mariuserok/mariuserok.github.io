About

I'm Marius. I've spent the better part of a decade in the unglamorous middle of software — the part where the deploy pipeline argues with the database and someone has to make peace.

I care about systems that stay boring under pressure: clear contracts, honest errors, observable everything. And when the backend is calm, I go make the frontend feel fast — because perceived performance is a feature too.

Outside of work I read too much about compilers, build silly shaders, and pretend my espresso setup is a distributed system.
