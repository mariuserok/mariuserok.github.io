About

I'm Marius — MART if you've only ever seen me in a commit log or a Teams thread. Software Engineer in IT Kort & Anlæg here at OK, based in Aarhus.

Roughly five years of software behind me before I took the engineer title here in December 2025. Most of that time has been spent on the unglamorous middle bit — where the pipeline argues with the database and somebody has to make peace.

These days it's portals, the platform underneath them, and an enthusiastic amount of poking at AI tooling to see what it can actually take off our plates. This whole page is one of those experiments.
